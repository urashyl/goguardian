What Parents & Students Should Know First

Most families assume school software just blocks bad websites. Static inspection of the GoGuardian extension (v4.1.246) shows it does significantly more:

1. Your child's screen can be captured every 3 seconds

Whenever a teacher session is active, GoGuardian streams screenshots of the browser every 3 seconds along with every open tab, page title, and how many milliseconds the student has been idle. Teachers can also request higher-resolution screenshots, lock the screen, close tabs (bypassing "unsaved changes" warnings), mute Google Meet, or draw directly over the screen.

Verified via LiveState v6 (LIVE_STATE_INTERVAL_MS = 3000), ExtScreenshots, Pusher client-shadow / client-annotate-screen-request, and goguardian-13.js clearing window.onbeforeunload.

2. At-home & weekend tracking is ON unless a school restricts it

At-home monitoring is not an opt-in feature that starts turned off—it is the opposite. GoGuardian runs 24/7 at home, at night, and on weekends unless a district explicitly enables school-hours or school-Wi-Fi restrictions. In the real-world policy inspected for this research, those restrictions were turned off, meaning 24/7 at-home monitoring was active—even on unmanaged or personal computers signed into the school Chrome profile.

Controlled by privacySettings.scheduling, monitorAtSchoolOnly, filterAtSchoolOnly, and onlyMonitorManagedDevices in GET /v1/policy.

3. Camera previews in a browser tab appear in screenshots

GoGuardian does not secretly turn on a laptop's webcam on its own, and Chrome blocks extensions from screenshotting the built-in Chromebook Camera app. However, if a student looks at themselves in any normal webpage (such as a Google Meet or Zoom preview, or a webcam website), live and periodic screenshots will capture their face and room.

background.js contains zero getUserMedia calls (webcam APIs exist only in WebRTC call pages); chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab() captures rendered http(s):// pixels while failing on chrome:// URLs.

4. Google Docs drafts and AI chatbot prompts are read

Even though Google Docs draws documents onto a canvas for performance, GoGuardian injects code that forces Google Docs to expose hidden text so drafts and titles can be scanned. It also captures prompts typed into ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, NotebookLM, and Google AI Mode.

goguardian-4.js sets _docs_force_html_by_ext; 19.js and server-pushed selectors in 22.js forward AI prompts to ai-events-intake.goguardian.com/v1/submit and Beacon.

5. Automated AI scores "off-task" reading & scans images

During class, the full visible text of whatever article or page the student is reading can be sent to an AI off-task scorer, while an on-device AI model scans images on webpages. If a Smart Alert triggers on a background tab, GoGuardian can briefly switch focus to that tab, snap a screenshot, and switch back.

Uses POST /v1/teacher/taskiness/predict, an 8-shard TF.js MobileNet model in goguardian-18.js, and chrome.tabs.update(..., { active: true }) inside [lib/screenshot].

6. What it does NOT do (clearing up myths)

GoGuardian is not a global password keylogger: it does not read what you type into standard password boxes on normal login pages, and it does not secretly turn on the webcam without a video call. (Visible unmasked passwords in a screenshot, or secrets pasted into an AI chatbot, would still be captured.)

1.js and 11.js inspect visible innerText/metadata rather than input.value; 19.js is scoped in manifest.json strictly to 7 AI/search origins.

Questions Parents Can Ask Their School District

  • After-hours & weekend rules: Are school-hours and school-IP scheduling limits enabled, or is monitoring running 24/7 at home?
  • Data retention: How long are captured screenshots, browsing logs, and AI chatbot prompts stored, and when are they deleted?
  • Who has access: Which staff members can view live student screens, receive safety/Smart Alerts, or inspect saved snapshots?
  • Personal home devices: If a student signs into Chrome with a school Google account on a family-owned PC or Mac, does the district restrict monitoring to managed Chromebooks only?
  • Transparency to families: Are parents and students notified in writing about Google Docs scanning, AI prompt logging, and live screen capture?

Manifest V3 Permissions manifest.json

Because GoGuardian is deployed via Chrome Enterprise policy on managed profiles, it retains privileged permissions—including webRequestBlocking inside Manifest V3.

  • <all_urls> host permissions + webRequest / webRequestBlocking
  • tabs, activeTab, webNavigation, scripting (including dynamic script injection)
  • enterprise.deviceAttributes (managed Chromebook Directory Device ID lookup)
  • management (enumerate, audit, and disable other installed Chrome extensions)
  • nativeMessaging (bridges to chromium.goguardian.ggwindows on Windows)
  • system.cpu & system.memory (hardware profiling)
  • offscreen (hidden DOM parser for Google account HTML scraping)
  • identity, identity.email, idle, alarms, notifications, unlimitedStorage

Advanced Technical Summary v4.1.246

  • Entity flushing: Tab state, titles, idle timers, and iframe URLs batch to Zephyr every 60s (or 1s under increased flush rate).
  • about:blank bypasses defeated: Dynamically injects goguardian-21.js (html2canvas-pro) for visual capture and uses webNavigation.getAllFrames to log embedded iframe URLs and Panther categories.
  • Extension control: Syncs all installed extensions via SyncInstalledExtensions and disables policy-blocked extensions via chrome.management.setEnabled.
  • 25-second watchdog: Pings content scripts across open tabs every 25 seconds to check script health.
